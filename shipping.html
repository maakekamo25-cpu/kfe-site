Shipping Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Quick summary

  • We deliver to: addresses in South Africa. Goods ship from China.
  • We place your order: within 2 business days of your payment being confirmed.
  • Packing and dispatch: usually 1 to 3 business days after we place the order.
  • Delivery: usually 12 to 60 days after dispatch. The estimate for each product is shown on the product page.
  • Overall: most orders arrive about 2 to 9 weeks after payment.
  • Tracking: we email you a tracking number as soon as your order ships.
  • Not arrived? If your order has not arrived 60 days after it shipped, contact us and we will replace it or refund you in full.

1. Where we ship

We deliver to addresses in South Africa only. Your goods are shipped from China by our fulfilment partner.

2. Order processing

When your payment is confirmed we place your order with our China fulfilment partner within 2 business days. The partner then packs and dispatches it, which usually takes 1 to 3 business days.

3. Delivery times

Delivery times depend on the product and the shipping method. Each product page shows its own estimate. Most are between 12 and 60 days from dispatch. Parcels are cleared through South African customs on arrival, which can add time. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

4. Shipping cost

Product prices do not include shipping. The shipping cost to your address in South Africa is shown in your cart, and added to your total, before you pay. We charge no VAT because we are not VAT registered. Import duty and VAT are not included: for some shipping methods, customs may charge them to you on delivery, for example if your parcel is inspected.

5. Tracking

We email you a tracking number when your order ships. You can also check your order at any time on our Track order page. Tracking can take a few days to start showing movement.

6. Orders with more than one item

Items in the same order may ship separately, in different parcels, and may arrive on different dates.

7. Your delivery address

Please check your address and mobile number carefully before you pay, because the carrier may need to contact you. We cannot change an address after an order has shipped. If a parcel is returned because the address was wrong or incomplete, we may charge the reasonable cost of sending it again.

8. Delays and lost parcels

Customs, carriers and weather can cause delays. If your order has not arrived 60 days after it shipped, contact us. We will follow it up with the carrier and, if it is lost, send a replacement or refund you in full.

9. Damaged or wrong items

Please check your parcel when it arrives and tell us as soon as possible (ideally within 48 hours), with photos. See our Returns and Refunds Policy.
