Returns and Refunds

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Changing your mind

You may cancel your order within 7 days after receiving the goods, without giving a reason and without penalty. The only charge is the direct cost of returning the goods to us. Some goods are excluded by law (for example, goods made to your specification). If you paid already, we will refund you in full within 30 days of the cancellation.

Cancelling before delivery

Contact us as soon as you can. If we have not yet ordered the goods from the supplier, we will cancel and refund you in full.

Faulty, damaged or wrong goods

If goods are defective, unsafe, damaged or not what you ordered, you may return them within 6 months of delivery and choose a repair, a replacement or a full refund. We pay for the return in these cases. Please check your parcel when it arrives and tell us as soon as possible (ideally within 48 hours), with photos.

Failed inspection

If goods fail our quality check in China, we will replace them or refund you in full.

How to return something

  1. Email nextdriveev1@gmail.com or call 067 772 2105 / 082 665 0024. Give your order reference (it starts with KFE-) and the reason.
  2. We will confirm the return address and steps.
  3. Send the goods back unused, in their original packaging where possible.

Refunds

Refunds are paid to the payment method you used, through PayFast. Your bank may take a few days to show the money.

This policy does not limit your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.
