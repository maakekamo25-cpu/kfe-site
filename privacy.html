Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who is responsible

NXTDRV EV (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2026/492988/07 (trading as KFE) is the responsible party. Our Information Officer is the company Director, reachable at nextdriveev1@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your name, email address, mobile number, delivery address, order details, and your South African ID or passport number. We never receive your card details, because PayFast handles payments.

Why we use it

To process and deliver your order, to verify the recipient on delivery, to complete customs clearance, to contact you about your order, to handle returns and complaints, and to meet legal and tax duties.

Your ID or passport number

We check the number is valid when you order and keep only its last four characters, to confirm identity on delivery. If customs clearance requires your full number, we will ask you for it separately.

Who we share it with

PayFast (payments); our China fulfilment partner, to whom we send your name, delivery address, phone number and email so they can ship your order (this means your information is sent outside South Africa); carriers and customs (delivery and clearance); our email provider (to send you order and shipping emails); and the hosting and technology providers that run our website and systems. We do not sell your information. We do not share your ID number with our fulfilment partner.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed to complete your order, handle returns and meet legal requirements (for example, financial records are kept for 5 years). After that we delete or anonymise it.

Security

Our website uses encrypted connections, payments are handled by PayFast, and access to personal information is limited to people who need it.

Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what we hold about you, correct it, delete it or stop using it, and you may withdraw your consent. Email nextdriveev1@gmail.com. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Our shop is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
